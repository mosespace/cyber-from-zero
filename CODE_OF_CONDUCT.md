# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Cyber From Zero to be a friendly, safe place to learn and to contribute, for everyone,
whatever their age, background, identity, experience, country or ability. Many people here are
beginners. Treating each other well is part of how we teach.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Welcome questions, including ones that seem basic. Everyone started at zero.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive and about the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and credit other people's work.
- Respect people's time. Maintainers and contributors are volunteers.
- Keep the project's ethics: **defence and education only**. Only test systems you own or have written permission to test.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discrimination of any kind
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information
- Pressuring or shaming someone for what they do not know
- Sharing or requesting material that helps attack real people, organisations or systems without permission
- Anything else that would be considered unprofessional in a classroom

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any place where
someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and
may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban people who do not follow it. We will act fairly
and, where we can, in private.

**To report a problem,** use GitHub's private "Report a vulnerability" or "Report content" tools on
this repository, or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
with discretion and the reporter will not be punished for reporting in good faith.
