Tools are just helpers. Understanding comes first. Use these **only on systems you own or have written permission to test**.

| Tool | Used for |
|------|----------|
| Nmap | Finding devices and open ports |
| Wireshark | Looking inside network packets |
| Burp Suite | Testing websites |
| Metasploit | Testing known weaknesses |
| John the Ripper / Hashcat | Testing password strength |
