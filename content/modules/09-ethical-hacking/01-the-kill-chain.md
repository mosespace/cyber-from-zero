Attacks follow steps. If defenders can break any one step, the attack fails.

```chart:kill-chain
```

## Plain English

1. **Recon**: collect public information about the target.
2. **Scanning**: look for open ports and weak software.
3. **Gain access**: use a weakness to get in.
4. **Stay in**: add a hidden way back.
5. **Move around**: go from one computer to the next.
6. **Reach the goal**: steal or damage data.
7. **Cover tracks**: hide what happened.

## Why defenders love this model

Each step leaves clues. If you notice scanning early, you can stop the attacker long before they steal anything.
