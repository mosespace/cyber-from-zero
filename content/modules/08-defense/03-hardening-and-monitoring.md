## Hardening: make it tougher

- Change default passwords.
- Remove programs and services you do not use.
- Turn on automatic updates (**patching**).
- Close unused ports.
- Use MFA and least privilege.
- Encrypt disks, especially on laptops.

## Monitoring: watch for trouble

**Logs** are diaries kept by computers: who logged in, what failed, what changed. Tools gather logs in one place called a **SIEM**.

Things that look suspicious:

- Many failed logins from one place
- A login at 3 a.m. from another country
- A user suddenly copying huge amounts of data
