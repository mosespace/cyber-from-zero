Think of a big apartment building.

- The **IP address** is the building's street address.
- The **port** is the apartment door number.

Data needs both to reach the right program.

## IP address types

| Type | Example | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| IPv4 | `192.168.1.10` | Four numbers from 0 to 255 |
| IPv6 | `2001:db8::1` | Much longer, made for the future |
| Private | `192.168.x.x` | Used inside homes and offices |
| Public | `93.184.216.34` | Visible on the internet |
