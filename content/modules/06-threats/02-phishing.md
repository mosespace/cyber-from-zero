**Phishing** is a fake email, text or website that pretends to be someone you trust, to trick you into giving away a password or money.

## Red flags

- Pressure: "Act now or your account will close!"
- Strange sender address: `support@paypa1-security.com`
- Generic greeting: "Dear customer"
- Links that do not match when you hover over them
- Attachments you did not expect
- Spelling mistakes or an offer that is too good to be true

## Types

| Name | Channel |
|------|---------|
| Phishing | Email |
| Smishing | Text message (SMS) |
| Vishing | Phone call (voice) |
| Spear phishing | Aimed at one specific person |

## What to do

1. Stop. Do not click.
2. Go to the real website by typing the address yourself.
3. Report the message.
4. If you did click and typed a password, change it right away and turn on MFA.

## Remember

- Slow down. Attackers want you rushed.
- When in doubt, check another way.
