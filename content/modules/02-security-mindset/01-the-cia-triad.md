Nearly everything in cybersecurity serves three goals. We call them the **CIA triad**. (It has nothing to do with the spy agency.)

```chart:cia-triad
```

## The three goals

- **Confidentiality**: only the right people can see the data. Example: only you can read your messages.
- **Integrity**: the data is correct and has not been secretly changed. Example: your bank balance does not change by itself.
- **Availability**: the data and services work when you need them. Example: a shop website that is not crashed on sale day.

## Match the attack to the goal

| Attack | Goal that is broken |
|--------|---------------------|
| Someone steals a database of emails | Confidentiality |
| A criminal edits a bank transfer amount | Integrity |
| Attackers flood a website until it crashes | Availability |
| Ransomware locks your files | Availability |

## Try it yourself

Pick an app you use every day. Write one sentence for each goal: how does the app protect it?

## Remember

- C: keep secrets secret.
- I: keep data correct.
- A: keep things working.
