Imagine your house.

- A **vulnerability** is a weakness: a window that does not lock.
- A **threat** is something that could use the weakness: a burglar.
- A **risk** is the chance that the bad thing happens, and how much it would hurt.

## Risk in one line

> Risk = how likely it is x how bad it would be

A small chance of a huge loss can be a bigger risk than a big chance of a tiny loss.

## What do we do about risk?

| Choice | Meaning | House example |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Reduce | Fix the weakness | Install a lock |
| Avoid | Stop doing the risky thing | Do not keep cash at home |
| Transfer | Let someone else carry it | Buy insurance |
| Accept | Live with it | Ignore a small scratch on the door |
