The word "hacker" simply means someone who is very good with computers. What they do with that skill is what matters.

## Types of hackers

| Type | Goal | Legal? |
|------|------|--------|
| White hat (ethical) | Find weaknesses so they can be fixed | Yes, with permission |
| Black hat | Steal, cheat or destroy | No |
| Grey hat | Break rules but without bad intent | Usually not |
| Script kiddie | Use tools made by others, for fun or fame | No |
| Hacktivist | Protest for a cause | No |
| Nation-state | Spy for a government | Not for the public |
