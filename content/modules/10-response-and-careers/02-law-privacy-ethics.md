Skill brings responsibility.

## The law

Most countries have computer misuse laws. Accessing a system without permission is a crime, even if you "just looked around" and even if you meant well.

## Privacy

Privacy laws such as GDPR in Europe say organisations must protect personal data, only collect what they need, and report serious leaks. Fines can be very large.

## Ethics checklist

- Do I have permission?
- Could this hurt someone?
- Am I being honest in my report?
