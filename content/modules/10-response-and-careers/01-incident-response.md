An **incident** is a security event that causes harm or could. Teams follow a clear plan so they do not panic.

## The six steps

1. **Prepare**: have a plan, contacts and tools ready.
2. **Identify**: confirm something is really wrong.
3. **Contain**: stop it spreading (for example, unplug the machine from the network).
4. **Eradicate**: remove the cause.
5. **Recover**: bring systems back safely.
6. **Learn**: write down what happened and improve.
