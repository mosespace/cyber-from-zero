A computer is a machine that follows instructions very fast. That is all. Your phone, your laptop, a game console and even a smart fridge are computers.

## The four main parts

| Part | Job | Real-life comparison |
|------|-----|----------------------|
| CPU (processor) | Does the thinking and maths | The chef |
| RAM (memory) | Holds things you use right now | The kitchen table |
| Storage (disk) | Keeps things for a long time | The pantry |
| Network card | Talks to other computers | The telephone |

## Hardware and software

- **Hardware** is the part you can touch: screen, keyboard, chips.
- **Software** is the set of instructions: apps, games, the operating system.

Hackers almost always attack the **software**, because it is written by humans and humans make mistakes.

## Why this matters for security

Every attack ends up doing one of three things: reading data, changing data, or stopping a computer from working. Knowing where data lives (RAM, disk, network) tells you where to protect it.

## Remember

- A computer follows instructions, nothing more.
- Software mistakes are called **bugs**. Some bugs become **vulnerabilities**.
- Security is the job of keeping computers doing only what their owners want.
