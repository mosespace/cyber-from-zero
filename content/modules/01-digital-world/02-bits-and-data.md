Computers do not understand letters or pictures. They only understand two states: **on (1)** and **off (0)**. One of these is called a **bit**. Eight bits make a **byte**.

## Counting with only two digits

We normally count in tens (0 to 9). Computers count in twos. This is called **binary**.

| Normal number | Binary |
|---------------|--------|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 10 |
| 3 | 11 |
| 4 | 100 |
| 5 | 101 |

## Letters are numbers too

The letter **A** is stored as the number 65, which is `01000001` in binary. A whole message is just a long list of numbers.

## Sizes you will meet

- 1 KB is about a short email.
- 1 MB is about one photo.
- 1 GB is about 250 songs.
