Every click is a **request** from your browser and a **response** from the server.

```chart:http-flow
```

## Parts of a request

| Part | Example |
|------|---------|
| Method | `GET` (read) or `POST` (send) |
| URL | `/login` |
| Headers | Extra info, like your browser type |
| Body | Form data such as your username |

## Status codes

- **200** OK
