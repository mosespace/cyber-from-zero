Phishing works when we are in a hurry. This lab slows you down on purpose.

## The practice message

> From: support@paypa1-security.com
> Subject: URGENT: your account will be closed in 24 hours
>
> Dear customer, we noticed strange activity. Click here to verify your identity now or lose access.

## Step 1: Check the sender

Look closely at the address. Is it `paypal.com` or something close? Here the letter **l** is replaced by the number **1**.

## Step 2: Check the pressure

Count the pressure words: URGENT, 24 hours, lose access. Real companies rarely threaten you like this.

## Step 3: Check the greeting

"Dear customer" means the sender does not know your name.

## Step 4: Check the link without clicking

On a computer, hover the mouse over the link. On a phone, press and hold. The real address appears. Does it match the company?
