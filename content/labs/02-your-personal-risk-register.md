Companies keep a **risk register**: a table of the things that could go wrong. You will make a small one for your own life. No tools are needed, only a pen or a notes app.

## Step 1: List what matters

Write down your five most valuable digital things. For example:

1. Your email account
2. Your phone
3. Your photos
4. Your bank app
5. Your school or work files

## Step 2: Name a threat for each

Think about what could go wrong. A stolen phone? A phishing email? A broken hard drive?

## Step 3: Score each one

Give **likelihood** and **impact** a score from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Multiply them.

| Asset | Threat | Likelihood | Impact | Risk |
|-------|--------|-----------:|-------:|-----:|
| Email | Phishing | 4 | 5 | 20 |
| Photos | Phone lost | 3 | 4 | 12 |
| Bank app | Weak PIN | 2 | 5 | 10 |

## Step 4: Choose a response

For each row choose one: **reduce**, **avoid**, **transfer** or **accept**. Write the exact action. For example: "Turn on MFA for my email."
