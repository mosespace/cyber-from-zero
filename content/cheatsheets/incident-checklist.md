---
title: Incident Response Checklist
summary: A calm list to follow when something goes wrong.
color: "#7ED3D9"
---
## First 15 minutes

- [ ] Write down the time and what you noticed.
- [ ] Do not panic or delete anything.
- [ ] Disconnect the affected device from the network.
- [ ] Tell the right person (IT, your manager, or your bank).

## Contain

- [ ] Change passwords from a clean device.
- [ ] Sign out all other sessions.
- [ ] Turn on MFA.
- [ ] Disable accounts that may be affected.

## Investigate

- [ ] What happened, and when did it start?
- [ ] Which accounts, files or devices are involved?
- [ ] Was data stolen, changed or locked?

## Recover

- [ ] Restore from a clean backup.
- [ ] Reinstall systems if you cannot trust them.
- [ ] Apply the missing updates.
- [ ] Watch closely for a week.

## Learn

- [ ] Write a short report with a timeline.
- [ ] List what worked and what did not.
