title: Incident Response Checklist
summary: A calm list to follow when something goes wrong.
color: "#7ED3D9"
---
## First 15 minutes

- [ ] Write down the time and what you noticed.
- [ ] Do not panic or delete anything.
- [ ] Disconnect the affected device from the network.
- [ ] Tell the right person (IT, your manager, or your bank).

## Contain

- [ ] Change passwords from a clean device.
- [ ] Sign out all other sessions.
- [ ] Turn on MFA.
- [ ] Disable accounts that may be affected.
