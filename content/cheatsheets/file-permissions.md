## The three powers

| Letter | Number | Power |
|--------|-------:|-------|
| r | 4 | Read |
| w | 2 | Write |
| x | 1 | Run |

Add the numbers for each group: **owner**, **group**, **everyone else**.

## Common combinations

| Number | Letters | Meaning |
|-------:|---------|---------|
| 7 | rwx | Everything |
| 6 | rw- | Read and write |
