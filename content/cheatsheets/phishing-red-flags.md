## The ten-second check

1. **Sender**: is the address exactly right?
2. **Pressure**: does it say urgent, final or act now?
3. **Greeting**: does it know your name?
4. **Link**: does the hover address match the company?
5. **Attachment**: were you expecting one?
6. **Request**: is it asking for a password, code or money?
7. **Spelling**: are there odd mistakes?
8. **Offer**: is it too good to be true?
9. **Channel**: would this company really contact you this way?
10. **Feeling**: do you feel rushed or scared?

## Types

| Name | Channel |
|------|---------|
| Phishing | Email |
| Smishing | Text message |
| Vishing | Phone call |
| Spear phishing | Targeted at one person |
